# Privacy Policy for VersaStack Everyday Toolkit

**Effective Date:** October 6, 2026

**VersaStack: Everyday Toolkit** ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our application operates and handles user information.

---

## 1. Information Collection and Use
VersaStack is designed as an offline utility tool. We **do not collect, store, transmit, or share** any personally identifiable information (PII) such as your name, email address, location, or device identifiers.

## 2. Local Data Storage
All calculations, counters, and preferences generated within the application are processed locally on your device. None of this data is uploaded to external servers or third-party services.

## 3. Third-Party Services
Our application does not integrate third-party analytics frameworks, advertising networks, or tracking SDKs that gather user data.

## 4. Device Permissions
VersaStack does not require sensitive permissions (such as Location, Camera, Contacts, or Microphone) to function.

## 5. Children's Privacy
Our application does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
